# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Target Market
* **Primary:** Solopreneurs, gig workers, and mobile service providers (plumbers, cleaners, tutors).
* **Secondary:** Digital freelancers (designers, writers) who need simple invoicing.

## 2. Acquisition Channels
* **Social Media (TikTok / Instagram Reels):** Short, punchy videos showing a vendor finishing a job and generating a receipt in 5 seconds on their phone.
* **SEO / Content Marketing:** Blog posts targeting long-tail keywords like "free receipt maker for WhatsApp", "offline invoice generator mobile".
* **Product-Led Growth (Viral Loop):** Every free receipt generated has a "Generated by VendorFlow" watermark. When a vendor sends a receipt to a client, the client discovers the app.

## 3. Pricing Strategy
* **Freemium Model:** Core features (invoicing, catalog, clients) are 100% free.
* **Ad-Supported:** Users can watch a short video ad to remove the watermark for a single receipt.
* **Premium Subscription:** A low monthly/yearly fee to permanently remove watermarks, unlock premium templates, and upload custom logos.

## 4. Launch Plan
* **Phase 1 (Soft Launch):** Release as a PWA. Share in niche communities (Reddit r/freelance, IndieHackers) to gather feedback and fix bugs.
* **Phase 2 (Marketing Push):** Launch on Product Hunt. Start social media ad campaigns.
* **Phase 3 (App Stores):** Wrap the PWA using Capacitor/TWA and launch on Google Play Store and Apple App Store for wider distribution.
