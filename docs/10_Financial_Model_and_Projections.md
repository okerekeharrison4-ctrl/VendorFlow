# Financial Model & Projections

## 1. Revenue Streams
* **Premium Subscriptions:** 
  * Target Price: $4.99/month or $49.99/year.
  * Conversion Goal: Convert 3-5% of active free users to Premium.
* **Ad Revenue (Rewarded Video):**
  * Estimated eCPM: $10 - $15 (Rewarded video ads typically have high eCPMs).
  * Usage: Users watch an ad to remove the watermark.

## 2. Cost Structure
* **Hosting & Infrastructure:** 
  * Currently very low due to client-side offline-first architecture.
  * Future costs: Firebase/Supabase for cloud sync, CDN costs for static assets.
* **Marketing & User Acquisition:**
  * Social media ads, influencer partnerships.
  * Target Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): < $2.00.
* **Development & Maintenance:**
  * Engineering time, third-party API costs (e.g., currency conversion APIs if added).

## 3. 12-Month Projections (Hypothetical)
* **Month 1-3:** Focus on organic growth. 1,000 MAU. Revenue: $50/mo (Ads).
* **Month 4-6:** Introduce Premium. 5,000 MAU. 100 Premium users. Revenue: $500/mo (Subs) + $200/mo (Ads).
* **Month 7-12:** Scale marketing. 20,000 MAU. 600 Premium users. Revenue: $3,000/mo (Subs) + $800/mo (Ads).

## 4. Key Financial Metrics to Track
* **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):** From Premium subscriptions.
* **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** Combined ad and sub revenue divided by total users.
* **LTV (Lifetime Value):** How long a premium user stays subscribed.
* **Churn Rate:** Percentage of users canceling Premium each month.
