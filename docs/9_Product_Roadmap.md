# Product Roadmap

## Q3 2026: Foundation & Core Experience (Current)
* ✅ Offline-first architecture with IndexedDB and robust Base64 `localStorage` fallback (resolving "full disk" quota errors).
* ✅ Core CRUD for Invoices, Clients, and Catalog.
* ✅ PDF and Image export with WhatsApp integration.
* ✅ Ad-supported watermark removal and Premium toggle (using custom animated modals).
* ✅ 6 Customizable Invoice Templates (stabilized layout with `tabular-nums`, `break-words`, and responsive flexbox to prevent distortion with large numbers/long names).
* ✅ Multi-currency support expanded (recently added Brazilian Real - BRL).
* ✅ Analytics Dashboard with visual charts (Recharts).
* ✅ Cash Flow Forecasting based on payment history.
* ✅ Command Palette (Cmd+K) for quick navigation.
* ✅ PWA (Progressive Web App) installation support.
* ✅ Google AdSense Integration (Native & Interstitial ads) with Ad Blocking Recovery and Error Protection.

## Q4 2026: Growth & Retention
* 🔄 **Cloud Sync:** Optional user accounts (Firebase Auth) to sync data across multiple devices.
* 🔄 **Webhooks / API:** Allow users to connect VendorFlow to Zapier or Make.com.
* 🔄 **Multi-Language Support:** Translate the UI into Spanish, Portuguese, and French.

## Q1 2027: Monetization & Expansion
* 📅 **Payment Gateway Integration:** Allow users to add a "Pay Now" button to their PDF invoices via Stripe or PayPal links.
* 📅 **Expense Tracking:** Allow users to log business expenses and scan physical receipts.
* 📅 **Native Mobile Apps:** Launch official iOS and Android apps in the app stores.

## Q2 2027: Enterprise & Teams
* 🔮 **Team Collaboration:** Allow multiple staff members to generate receipts under one business account.
* 🔮 **Advanced Tax Reporting:** Generate automated tax reports based on local jurisdictions.
* 🔮 **Inventory Management:** Basic stock tracking tied to the Catalog.
