# User Personas

## Persona 1: Freelancer Fiona
* **Role:** Graphic Designer / Consultant
* **Demographics:** 25-35 years old, tech-savvy.
* **Pain Points:** Hates complex accounting software like QuickBooks. Needs to send a quick, beautiful invoice right after finishing a gig.
* **Goals:** Wants to look professional to clients. Needs to know when she gets paid.
* **Use Case:** Uses VendorFlow to generate a PDF invoice with the "Minimal" template and emails it to her corporate clients.

## Persona 2: Small Business Sam (Local Vendor)
* **Role:** Plumber / Electrician / Market Seller
* **Demographics:** 30-50 years old, uses mobile phone for everything.
* **Pain Points:** Often works in areas with bad internet. Needs to give customers a receipt immediately on the spot.
* **Goals:** Wants a fast, no-nonsense way to record sales and send proof of payment.
* **Use Case:** Uses VendorFlow entirely offline. Generates a receipt, hits "Share to WhatsApp", and sends the image directly to the customer's phone.

## Persona 3: Side-Hustle Sarah
* **Role:** Instagram Baker / Crafter
* **Demographics:** 20-40 years old, highly visual.
* **Pain Points:** Manages many small orders. Needs to keep track of her product catalog and prices.
* **Goals:** Wants her receipts to match her cute brand aesthetic.
* **Use Case:** Upgrades to Premium to add her bakery logo. Uses the "Elegant" template. Uses the Catalog feature to quickly add "Custom Cake" and "Cupcake Dozen" to invoices.
