# Messaging & Tone of Voice Guide

## 1. Brand Personality
VendorFlow is:
* **Empowering:** We help small businesses look big.
* **Simple:** We avoid accounting jargon. We use plain language.
* **Reliable:** We assure users their data is safe and always accessible.
* **Fast:** We emphasize speed and efficiency.

## 2. Tone of Voice
* **Professional yet approachable:** "Your receipt is ready" instead of "Transaction finalized."
* **Encouraging:** "Great job! Payment recorded."
* **Clear and concise:** Mobile screens have limited space. Keep copy short.

## 3. Core Messages & Value Propositions
* **Elevator Pitch:** "VendorFlow is the fastest way to create, manage, and share professional receipts right from your phone, even offline."
* **Value Prop 1 (Speed):** "Generate a receipt in seconds and send it straight to WhatsApp."
* **Value Prop 2 (Professionalism):** "Look like a pro with customizable templates and your own logo."
* **Value Prop 3 (Privacy):** "Your data stays on your device. Secure, encrypted, and offline-ready."

## 4. Copywriting Guidelines
* **Buttons:** Use action-oriented verbs (e.g., "Share Receipt", "Save Client", "Upgrade").
* **Error Messages:** Be helpful, not robotic. (e.g., "Oops, we couldn't save that. Please try again." instead of "Error 500: Save failed.")
* **Upsells:** Focus on value. (e.g., "Remove watermarks and add your logo with Premium.")
