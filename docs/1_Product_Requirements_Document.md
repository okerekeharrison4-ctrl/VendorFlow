# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
**Name:** VendorFlow
**Description:** A mobile-optimized, offline-first web application designed for freelancers, gig workers, and small business owners to easily create, manage, and share professional invoices and receipts.

## 2. Target Audience
* Freelancers and independent contractors.
* Small business owners and local vendors.
* Service providers who need to generate receipts on the go.

## 3. Core Features
* **Invoice & Receipt Generation:** Create professional invoices with multiple templates (Modern, Classic, Minimal, Bold, Elegant, Corporate).
* **Client Management:** Save and manage client details for quick invoicing.
* **Catalog Management:** Save products/services with prices for quick addition to invoices.
* **Offline-First Storage:** Data is stored locally using IndexedDB with AES-GCM encryption (fallback to localStorage).
* **Export & Sharing:** Export as PDF or Image. Direct sharing to WhatsApp, Email, or device sharing menu.
* **Premium Mode:** Remove "Generated by VendorFlow" watermark and add custom business logos.
* **Ad-Supported Free Tier:** Users can watch a rewarded video ad to temporarily remove watermarks. Interstitial ads are also shown periodically.
* **Backup & Sync:** Manual JSON export and import of all data.
* **Multi-Currency Support:** Support for global currencies including USD, EUR, NGN, INR, BRL, etc.
* **Analytics Dashboard:** Visual charts showing revenue, top clients, and best-selling items using Recharts.
* **Cash Flow Forecasting:** Predicts future payment dates based on average time-to-pay history.
* **Command Palette:** Quick navigation via Cmd+K/Ctrl+K shortcut.
* **PWA Support:** Installable as a Progressive Web App for a native-like mobile experience.

## 4. Success Metrics
* Daily Active Users (DAU) and Monthly Active Users (MAU).
* Number of invoices/receipts generated per user.
* Conversion rate from Free to Premium.
* Ad completion rate for watermark removal.

## 5. Future Scope
* Cloud synchronization across multiple devices.
* Direct payment gateway integrations (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).
* Expense tracking and basic tax reporting.
