# Business Plan / Pitch Deck Outline

## 1. The Problem
Micro-businesses, gig workers, and freelancers are underserved by traditional accounting software. Tools like QuickBooks or Xero are too complex, too expensive, and require a constant internet connection. These workers just need to send a quick, professional receipt from their phone.

## 2. The Solution: VendorFlow
A lightning-fast, mobile-optimized, offline-first web app that lets anyone generate a beautiful invoice or receipt in under 10 seconds and share it directly to WhatsApp.

## 3. Market Opportunity
The gig economy is exploding globally. There are hundreds of millions of informal merchants, freelancers, and independent contractors worldwide who rely on WhatsApp for business communication but lack professional invoicing tools.

## 4. Product Demo / Features
* Works completely offline.
* 6 professional templates.
* 1-click share to WhatsApp.
* Built-in catalog and client management.

## 5. Business Model
* **Freemium:** Free to use with a watermark.
* **Ad-Supported:** Watch a video to remove the watermark.
* **Premium:** $4.99/mo for custom logos and permanent watermark removal.

## 6. Go-To-Market Strategy
* Viral loop: Every free receipt sent is an advertisement for VendorFlow.
* Targeted social media marketing (TikTok, Instagram).
* App Store Optimization (ASO) once wrapped as a native app.

## 7. Competitive Advantage
* **Speed:** No mandatory account creation or complex onboarding.
* **Offline-First:** Works in areas with poor connectivity.
* **Mobile-Native Feel:** Designed specifically for one-handed mobile use.
