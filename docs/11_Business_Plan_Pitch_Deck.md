# Business Plan (Text Outline)

1. **Problem**: SMB billing tools are bloated, unsecure, or expensive.
2. **Solution**: VendorFlow gives instant invoicing, local-first speeds, but cloud-native durability securely via Firebase.
3. **Market**: 70M+ independent contractors globally.
4. **Advantage**: Lightning-fast, hyper-secure React/Vite/Firebase stack preventing local data leaks.
